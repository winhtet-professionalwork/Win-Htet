About Me

My name is Win Htet. I am a senior student at Loras College in Dubuque, Iowa, pursuing a double major in Mechanical Engineering and Mathematics.

I was born and raised in a small rural community in Myanmar. Growing up with limited access to educational resources helped shape my appreciation for education and its ability to create opportunities. After interruptions to my education, I continued pursuing my academic goals in the United States.

My academic interests focus on understanding engineering systems through mathematical modeling, experimentation, and computational methods. I am particularly interested in sensors and mechatronics, dynamic systems and control, computational mechanics, robotics, and renewable energy.

Beyond academics, I hope to use engineering, mathematics, and education to develop practical solutions and create opportunities for communities with limited resources.
