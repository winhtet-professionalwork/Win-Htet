Research Overview

I am interested in applying engineering principles, mathematical modeling, and computational methods to understand complex physical systems and solve practical engineering problems.

My academic interests connect mechanical engineering with applied mathematics, experimentation, simulation, and technology development.

Research Areas

1. Sensors and Mechatronics

Sensor systems, instrumentation, data acquisition, embedded systems, and intelligent monitoring.

2. Dynamic Systems and Control

Mathematical modeling, system dynamics, feedback control, and automation.

3. Computational Mechanics

Numerical methods, finite element analysis, computational modeling, and simulation of engineering systems.

4. Robotics and Automation

Mechanical system design, motion control, sensing, and automated systems.

5. Renewable Energy Systems

Solar energy applications, energy-efficient technologies, and sustainable engineering.

6. Thermal and Fluid Systems

Heat transfer, fluid mechanics, computational fluid dynamics, and thermal system analysis.

Future Research Goals

I hope to pursue graduate research in Mechanical Engineering and contribute to the development of practical engineering technologies through analytical, experimental, and computational approaches.

I welcome research opportunities, academic collaboration, advice, and mentorship.
